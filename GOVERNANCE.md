# Governance

## Project stewardship

Eye Voice was created by **Mostafa Medhat**, who serves as founder and lead maintainer. The lead maintainer is responsible for the product vision, accessibility principles, roadmap, releases, repository administration, and final merge decisions.

## Contributions

Community contributions are welcomed through issues and pull requests. Contributors are credited in Git history and may be acknowledged in release notes. A contribution does not transfer ownership of the project name, official distribution, or original project assets.

## Decision process

- Small fixes may be reviewed and merged directly by a maintainer.
- New features should begin with an issue describing the user need.
- Accessibility, privacy, and safety take priority over convenience.
- Breaking architectural or product changes require approval from the lead maintainer.
- If consensus cannot be reached, the lead maintainer makes the final decision and documents the reasoning.

## Becoming a maintainer

Consistent contributors may be invited to triage issues or review pull requests. Maintainer access is granted by the lead maintainer based on sustained, constructive participation and alignment with the project's accessibility and privacy values.

## Project identity

The source code is available under the MIT License. The **Eye Voice** project name, visual identity, and official project channels are stewarded by Mostafa Medhat. Forks should clearly state that they are community forks and not the official distribution.
